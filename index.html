Welcome to Hewad Foundation

Hewad Foundation is a nonprofit initiative dedicated to supporting Afghan refugees around the world.
Our mission is to provide hope, dignity, and essential support to families and individuals affected by migration and crisis.

🌍 How to Donate from Outside Afghanistan

If you live in Europe, the USA, Canada, or other countries and want to support the Hewad Charity Foundation, please use one of the following methods:

💳 Recommended Methods:

  • MoneyGram: Send funds to Afghanistan under the name of our local representative
  • Western Union: Directly send to the designated recipient in Kabul
  • Bank Transfer: If available, from European countries

📇 Recipient Details:

  • Recipient Name: Ahmadullah (subject to change depending on project)
  • Location: Kabul, Afghanistan
  • Phone / WhatsApp: +93 XXXXXXXX

After sending your donation, please share a photo or code of the transfer receipt with us via email or WhatsApp. We deeply appreciate your generosity and support.
