About Hewad Foundation

Hewad Foundation is a nonprofit organization founded with the mission of supporting Afghan refugees across the globe. Our goal is to create a network of hope and practical assistance by connecting those in need with generous volunteers and donors.

From providing emergency aid, food, and shelter, to helping children access education and families gain legal support, our foundation works tirelessly to restore dignity and opportunity to displaced individuals and communities.

We believe that everyone deserves a second chance—and together, we can build a future full of light, dignity, and opportunity.
